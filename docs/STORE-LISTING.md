## Full description (Play, up to 4000 characters)

AirSea Radar shows live air traffic and live marine traffic together on one fast, interactive radar map.

✈ LIVE FLIGHT TRACKER
• Thousands of aircraft worldwide, updated in real time and coloured by altitude
• Search any flight number (BA117, EK202, PK785), callsign, registration or aircraft code
• Tap a plane: airline, route and progress, ETA, altitude, speed, climb rate, aircraft type, photo, altitude chart
• Follow mode keeps the plane centred as it flies

🚢 LIVE SHIP TRACKER (AIS)
• Cargo ships, tankers, ferries, fishing boats, tugs and yachts, coloured by type
• Search by ship name, MMSI or IMO number
• Destination, ETA, speed, course, heading, navigational status, size and draught

🗺 BUILT-IN OFFLINE WORLD MAP
• The detailed world map is included in the app, so it opens instantly and works without a connection
• 5,000+ airports and 25,000 cities; dark, light, street and satellite maps when you're online

✨ MORE
• Airport panel: arriving and departing aircraft nearby, local time, day or night
• Day & night overlay, live weather radar, 3D globe
• Live statistics: flights by altitude, busiest airlines, the highest and fastest aircraft in view
• Filters by altitude, aircraft type, airline and military aircraft

Data from community ADS-B and AIS receiver networks (OpenSky Network, adsb.lol, aisstream.io,
Digitraffic) and open map data (Natural Earth, GeoNames, OurAirports). Coverage depends on receivers
and is best near land.

## Before publishing

- Privacy policy URL (the app shows ads and can use location)
- Data safety: advertising ID and app interactions (AdMob), approximate location (optional, on device only)
- app-ads.txt on your developer website (from AdMob)
- Avoid naming competitor brands (e.g. "Flightradar24", "MarineTraffic") in the title or description: both stores reject keyword stuffing with other brands' trademarks
