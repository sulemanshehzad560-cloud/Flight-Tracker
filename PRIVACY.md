# AirSea Radar privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

AirSea Radar is a live flight and ship tracker for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## No accounts
The app has no sign-up and no accounts. Your settings (map style, units, filters) are stored only on your phone.

## Location (optional)
If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location only to centre the map on where you are. Your location is used on your device and is not sent to us or stored on any server we run. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings; the app works without it.

## Flight, ship and map data
To show live traffic, the app requests public data for the area on screen from open sources (OpenSky Network, adsb.lol, airplanes.live, adsb.fi, adsbdb.com, planespotters.net, aisstream.io, Digitraffic, RainViewer, CARTO and Esri). Like any internet request, these services see your IP address and the map area being requested. No personal information about you is sent to them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
AirSea Radar is free and shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and where else the law requires it) the app asks for your consent first with Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in **Settings → Ad privacy settings**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Diagnostics
The app keeps a local log of crashes and errors on your phone. It is sent only if you choose **Settings → Send diagnostics report**, and then only by the email or app you pick. Google Play may also collect crash reports as described in its own policies.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data deletion
All app data is stored on your device. Delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

We do not sell your data.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
